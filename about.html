About Us

Growing the Kingdom

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you."

Matthew 28:19–20

We are a mobilized group of youth with a burning passion for Jesus and his mission to grow the kingdom. We are unnapologetic about our faith and committed to sharing the gospel with others. We meet three times a week on TikTok to pray, plan, share testimonies, win souls and encourage one another.
